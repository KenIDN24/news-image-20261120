# Sources - Friday 20 November 2026

Every story on this page was reported on, or dominated the papers
of, Friday 20 November 2026, cross-checked against wire, broadsheet
or broadcaster coverage of that day. Compiled 20 Nov 2026.
The page carries 14 stories: 2 leads, 3 panels, 6 briefs, and a
footer strip. Figures are as reported.

## Leads

**1. Trump, Xi and Putin hold first trilateral summit** - The
leaders of the United States, China and Russia met in Shenzhen on
the sidelines of APEC, their first-ever three-way meeting, and
signed a four-point "peace declaration": UN reform, increasing
refined-petroleum supply, limits on ICC jurisdiction / state
immunity, and reaffirming nuclear war must never be fought.
Ukraine was made a priority, per envoy Steve Witkoff.
- Reuters, Le Monde, TASS via StockBiz, 19-20 Nov.

**2. ICJ: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya** -
The International Court of Justice ruled 9-6 that Myanmar
committed genocide against the Rohingya in northern Rakhine in
2017 (about 10,000 killed), the first time a state has been held
responsible for genocide. It ordered reparations and the safe,
voluntary return of the displaced. Verdict 19 Nov; reactions and
front pages 20 Nov.
- Reuters, UN News, CNN, Le Monde, WSJ, 19-20 Nov.

## Panels (3-5)

**3. Russia strikes Kyiv with ballistic missiles** - Russia
attacked Kyiv with ballistic missiles overnight into 20 Nov,
with explosions and fires in several districts. Separately the
UK pledged over 1,000 drones for Ukraine in a GBP 260 million
package (Andy Burnham, Berlin).
- h.ua / Daily Baku, 20 Nov 01:22 UTC; AppiSToS wire, 20 Nov.

**4. Anthropic IPO is the biggest ever** - Anthropic, the first
frontier AI company to go public, priced at $83 a share, raised
about $81 billion at a valuation of about $1.5 trillion, and
closed up 17% at about $97 on its Nasdaq debut (ticker ANT).
- Reuters, AFP, Asianet, SimpleLife, 19-20 Nov.

**5. COP31 draft drops fossil fuels** - A revised COP31 draft
in Antalya, ahead of the Friday deadline, stripped all mention
of fossil fuels (oil, gas, coal) and dropped the $120 billion
adaptation-finance target, keeping a 35%-by-2035 electrification
goal and tripling renewables. Campaigners called it shocking.
- Reuters, IPS, PINA, 19-20 Nov.
